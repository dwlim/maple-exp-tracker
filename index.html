How potion usage is tracked

Counts update every 10 seconds. Usage measures observed decreases; refills preserve totals. Moving or replacing stacks can look like use, so reset usage after inventory changes. Rates start after 30 seconds and exclude pauses and long gaps. Unreadable quantities keep the last count; missing digits are never treated as zero. Assignments and totals are saved separately for 8- and 12-slot layouts. New session resets usage for both layouts.

Stream-card costs are estimates: observed items/hour × mesos/item. Defaults use MeowDB NPC buy prices, checked October 25, 2026. Edit each tracked slot’s price for your shop or Free Market purchase; enter 0 for free items. Unknown prices stay blank. HP + MP items appear separately to avoid counting their cost twice.

Is this safe?

The tracker runs locally in your browser, with no application backend. Your shared frames and EXP readings are processed on your device and are not uploaded. The OCR fallback may download model files from this site; recognition stays on your device.

You control screen sharing

Maple Track uses your browser's built-in screen-sharing picker. You choose what to share each time. Choose only the MapleStory window to keep other windows out of the capture. Stop sharing with the app or browser controls, or close this tab.

No access to game internals

The tracker reads visible pixels, like looking at a screenshot. It does not read or modify game memory or files, inject code, or send keyboard or mouse input to the game.

No installation, login, or audio

Browser mode needs no download, extension, administrator access, or MapleStory credentials. Audio capture is disabled. Text recognition is bundled with the page and runs on your device.

Your session stays in this browser

Readings and calibration settings are saved in this browser's local storage. They are not synced to an account or server. You can remove them by clearing this site's browser data.

Open source and easy to inspect

The source code is public so you can check how capture and recognition work. There are no analytics or advertising scripts. This is a community project, unaffiliated with Nexon.
